Designed Zyva, a health app (iOS, Android), from visual concepts in Figma to the UI of the native apps.

Overview

Zyva brings blood tests, DNA and wearables into one app: upload a lab report, scan a supplement and ask an AI assistant about the results. I designed the app and built its UI for iOS and Android together with the backend developers.

Design and build

I started in Figma with visual directions and the key screens. Development began in React Native, then the CTO moved the project to Kotlin Multiplatform with native UI, and I built the screens for iOS and Android in parallel with AI tools. As in PlanDesk, I set up specs for buttons, type, colours and spacing, so both apps look and behave the same.

Status

The app is in TestFlight and can be installed from zyva.health. Development is partly on hold, so the current build may differ from these screens.
