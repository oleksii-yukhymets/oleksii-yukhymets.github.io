RVA Fintech Solutions · 2022 – 2023

Designed a CRM admin panel for a Swiss payment system (web) — 8 sections and 80+ screens in 3 months on the MUI React design system.

Overview

An in-house admin panel for a Swiss payment system: currency volumes and exchange, users, sessions, balances and transactions in one place.

Role
UX/UI Designer
Team
CTO, business analyst, product & project managers, developers
Timeline
~3 months
Platform
Web · Admin panel

Challenges

  1. Create the company’s own admin panel.
  2. Work with a large volume of data and complex business processes.
  3. Hit a tight deadline — we needed a way to design and develop fast.

Solution

  1. Worked closely with the CTO, business analyst, product manager, project manager and the development team.
  2. Ran brainstorming sessions, then moved through wireframes and prototypes.
  3. Proposed the standard MUI React design system to speed up both design and front-end development.

Results

The interface was designed in about 3 months: 8 main sections and 80+ screens.
