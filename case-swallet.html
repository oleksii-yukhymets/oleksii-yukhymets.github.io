Full UX/UI redesign of S-Wallet, a crypto and fiat wallet (iOS, Android). New staking, interactive charts and asset tools lifted the app rating from 3.8 to 4.8.

Overview

S-Wallet is a multi-currency wallet for crypto and fiat on iOS and Android: users store, send, receive and exchange assets securely. To stay competitive in a fast-moving fintech market, the product needed better usability, new financial features and a modern interface.

My role

I was responsible for a full-scale redesign and functional expansion of the app, based on analytics and user feedback.

  1. User research & UX strategy. Market and competitor analysis to find key pain points and opportunities.
  2. Wireframing & prototyping. User flows, wireframes and interactive prototypes for new features and clearer navigation.
  3. Modernizing the UI. A new visual style with dark and light themes.
  4. Feature expansion. Staking, interactive price charts and better asset management tools.
  5. Iterating on feedback. Improved onboarding, transaction flows and portfolio management based on real user insights.

Results
